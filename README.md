## Selected Projects

- [**Monte Carlo Pricing of Barrier Options**](https://github.com/alansampaolo/efficient-monte-carlo-pricing-barrier-options) — *MSc thesis, MATLAB*
  Prices barrier options under Ornstein–Uhlenbeck stochastic volatility, comparing Euler, VHP and inverse Gaussian schemes with antithetic, control variate and conditional Monte Carlo estimators against Fourier benchmark prices. The inverse Gaussian scheme reduced pricing error about 20-fold versus Euler, and control variates cut standard errors by up to two orders of magnitude.

- [**Credit Risk Classification**](https://github.com/alansampaolo/credit-risk-classification) — *Python*
  End-to-end default prediction pipeline on Lending Club loan-level data, with SMOTE applied within stratified 5-fold cross-validation to avoid data leakage. Compares seven models on a held-out test set: logistic regression performs best (ROC-AUC 0.69; PR-AUC 0.35 vs. a 0.20 default rate). Explainability and robustness assessed with SAFE AI metrics.

- [**ESG, CDS Spreads & Counterparty CVA**](https://github.com/alansampaolo/eco-risk-alpha-cva) — *Python*
  Tests whether ESG characteristics add predictive information for CDS spreads beyond financial fundamentals, using econometric and machine learning models, and assesses the impact on counterparty CVA.

- [**ESG Risk Prediction**](https://github.com/alansampaolo/esg-risk-prediction) — *Python*
  Merges financial and ESG datasets and compares statistical and machine learning models to identify the factors most predictive of corporate ESG risk scores, with an LLM called via API to generate automatic reports on the results.

- [**Mortgage Optimization**](https://github.com/alansampaolo/mortgage-optimization-linear-programming) — *Python, Gurobi*
  Linear program that finds the optimal allocation across multiple mortgage options, minimising monthly payments under borrowing and repayment constraints, with dual and sensitivity analysis.
